    Sites

    Water withdrawn · GRI 303-3

    Where the water comes from

    Category 1 and 2 are freshwater ("high quality" in the ICMM guide). Category 3 is low quality, here almost all seawater.

    Split by
    Water discharged and consumed · GRI 303-4, 303-5

    Where the water goes

    Discharge goes back to rivers, groundwater or the sea. Consumption is water that does not come back: evaporated, held in tailings or used up.

    ICMM 2021 · site water balance

    Does water in equal water out?

    Withdrawal − discharge − change in storage should equal consumption. We allow ±5%. Pick a site above or below.
