One number for many mines

Reports give one water total for the whole company. Nobody can see which mine uses the water.

Seawater and freshwater mixed

Only freshwater competes with towns, farms and rivers, yet both sit in the same total.

Numbers that don't add up

Water in should equal water out. In published reports it often doesn't, and nobody checks.

How it works

From report to answers in three steps

  1. 1

    Bring the data in

    Import the company's sustainability data file, or type the figures for a site into a short form. Both end up in the same site account.

  2. 2

    Report and check

    Every site gets the same account: freshwater and seawater, sources, discharge, consumption and recycling, checked against the ICMM industry rules.

  3. 3

    Look ahead

    See each site's trend and a forecast to 2030, and test a sustainable option such as recycling more water.

What the dashboard answers
