      The form is generated from mine-water-passport.schema.json. Validation, mass balance (inputs − outputs − Δstorage ≈ 0) and the transparency score update as you type. Tier 1 = 12 must-report data points; switch to Tier 2 for the full balance.

      Report extractor – backfill passports from existing reports

      Paste a paragraph from a sustainability report. A deterministic extractor pulls water figures, normalises units to megalitres, detects the year, and flags confidence. This is the seed (and the verifier) of an LLM-assisted pipeline that backfills thousands of sites from public reports.

      The MineWater Passport standard (draft v0.1)

      A minimum site-level reporting format built on ICMM (2021) and the MCA Water Accounting Framework, with every field mapped to GRI 303 / GRI 14, ESRS E3, CDP and IRMA. The crosswalk below is read live from the schema’s machine-readable annotations.

      Comparability

      Site level, not company level. Normalised intensities (m³/t ore, m³/t metal or LCE with explicit allocation). Consumption and withdrawal always reported separately, with WAF water-quality categories.

      Minimal burden

      Tier 1 = 12 data points any site can report in a day. Tier 2 = full WAF-style balance. Basin context is derived from coordinates – not asked of the reporter.

      Decision relevance

      Every figure carries a data-quality flag. Basin stress, competing users and monthly profiles turn totals into impact signals for buyers, basin authorities and researchers.

      Open data

      Open JSON Schema, open validator, CC-BY dataset. One passport satisfies GRI, ESRS, CDP and IRMA requests – “report once, reuse everywhere”.

        Quality categories (MCA WAF)

        Cat 1High quality, minimal treatment (ICMM “high”)
        Cat 2Medium quality, moderate treatment (ICMM “low”)
        Cat 3Low quality: high TDS, metals, extreme pH (ICMM “low”)

        ICMM high/low split vs WAF Cat 1/2/3 is our crosswalk convention; GRI 303 “freshwater” (≤1,000 mg/L TDS) is a separate flag per flow.
